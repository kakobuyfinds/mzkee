What is a
Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize items for shopping research. Entries may include product links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. Treat each entry as a starting point for comparison: a spreadsheet does not guarantee product quality, authenticity or availability.

Kakobuy shopping guide

A better shortlist
in four steps.

A long list is easy to collect. A useful list helps you decide. Give every saved product a purpose, a measurement check and a clear cost estimate.

Browse categories on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose one category first

    Start with shoes, a layering piece or an accessory. Set your preferred color, intended use and spending limit before collecting links.

  2. Save the exact listing

    Record the product URL, selected variant and date checked. Similar photographs do not necessarily represent the same item, materials or seller.

  3. Compare measurements and photographs

    Match the size chart to something you own. Where available, review recent item-specific photographs for shape, finish and visible construction details.

  4. Calculate the complete cost

    Check item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the return terms before paying.

Clear answers before you browse

A few useful
clarifications.

Understand what a product list can tell you and which details still need checking.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize items for shopping research. Entries may include product links, categories, prices, sizes and notes. A spreadsheet is a starting point for comparison, not a guarantee of quality, authenticity or availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide and links to CAFANS product categories. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or verify live Kakobuy listings.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, shoulder, length and waist measurements. For shoes, compare foot length and the seller's size chart instead of relying only on regional size labels.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties before paying.

Are the brand images verified CAFANS inventory?

No. The photographs are editorial references. They do not establish CAFANS inventory, product authenticity, seller authorization or an affiliation with Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it is not an official Kakobuy service.
